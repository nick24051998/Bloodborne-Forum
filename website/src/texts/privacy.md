This notice covers our website (bbport.de) and our forum (forum.bbport.de).
Our Discord server is a separate service; Discord's own privacy policy applies there.

## Who is responsible

The Bloodborne PC Port Community, a volunteer project. Contact for all privacy questions:
[bbport@supermedo.com](mailto:bbport@supermedo.com).

## Website

**Hosting.** The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub
processes technical data such as your IP address, the page you request, the time and your browser's
user agent, to deliver the site and protect it against attacks. We have a legitimate interest in a
secure and working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub processes this data in the USA; see the
[GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
for the safeguards it uses.

**No cookies, no tracking.** The website sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and uses no
analytics or advertising. Fonts and images are served from the website itself, not from third parties.

**"Will it run on my PC?"** The check in the install guide runs entirely in your browser; your choices are not
stored or sent to us. Only if you click Detect does your browser tell the page the name of your graphics card, to
pick it in the list. Your choices appear in the page address so you can share the result; like any address, it
reaches GitHub only when you open or share that link.

**Server page.** The [Server](/server/) page, its hunter pages and the band on the home page show
statistics from SuperMedo Server, the online server for the port run by Supermedo. While a page is open,
your browser fetches them from our forum (forum.bbport.de) about once a minute; the forum in turn asks
game.supermedo.com at most once a minute for everyone, so SuperMedo Server never sees your address for
this (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The statistics are the ones SuperMedo Server publishes: totals, and players
by their Online ID with their in-game records, never where they are right now. Players can have their account
hidden: ask the team on our Discord, they pass it on to SuperMedo Server.

**BB Report Tool.** If you allow it once in the tool, the BB Report Tool on your PC downloads its list of known
problems and fixes from bbport.de/tool/ (or from the forum, if bbport.de can't be reached) when it starts. It only
downloads: it sends nothing about you, your PC or your report. As with any page of this website, GitHub or our forum
server sees your IP address for the download (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, your consent). You can switch it off in the tool
at any time. The report itself stays on your PC until you post it yourself.

**Links.** Links to Discord, GitHub, The Hunter's Dream and other sites take you to services with their
own privacy policies. Nothing from them is loaded before you click.

## Forum

**Server and connection.** The forum runs on our own server in Germany. Connections reach it through
Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA), which protects it against attacks and processes connection data
such as your IP address for that purpose (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See the
[Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for its safeguards. Our server
writes technical log files (IP address, time, requested page, browser); they are rotated automatically
and overwritten after a short time.

**Logging in with Discord.** Forum accounts are created with "Log in with Discord". Discord then sends us
your Discord user ID, username, avatar and e-mail address. We use them to create and run your account
(Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Your e-mail address is not shown to other members.

**Your profile on our Discord server.** While your account is linked, the forum regularly reads your
nickname, profile picture and roles on our Discord server, and whether you are banned there. Your forum name
and picture follow that profile, unless you upload your own picture in the forum. Team roles show as badges,
all other roles stay hidden and only control access. A ban on our Discord server suspends your forum account,
and a forum suspension also bans you from our Discord server until it ends. We do this to keep one community
with the same names and rules in both places (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**What you post.** Discussions, posts, likes and your profile are visible to everyone who visits the
forum, including search engines. Files you upload (images, logs, zip/7z archives) can only be opened by
logged-in members. Report zips and logs can contain information about your PC, so please check
them before uploading and don't post personal data you don't want to share. When you post, the forum
stores the IP address you used, so moderators can act against spam and abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Discord announcements.** New public discussions are announced in a channel of our Discord server with
their title, the author's name, tags, the beginning of the first post and a link. This tells the community
about new topics (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Discord's privacy policy applies there.

**Cookies.** The forum sets only cookies it needs to work: a session cookie that keeps you logged in
while you browse, and, if you choose "Remember me", a cookie that keeps you logged in longer. It also
remembers a few choices you make, such as hiding the welcome banner, in your browser. None of this is
used for tracking.

**Live statistics.** The forum shows numbers from SuperMedo Server, the online co-op server. Our server
fetches them directly from game.supermedo.com; no data about you is sent there.

**"My game account."** When you click "My game account" in your forum menu, the forum sends your Discord user ID
to SuperMedo Server (game.supermedo.com), with a signature that is valid for one minute, so it can sign you in to
your game account there (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, at your request). Nothing else is sent, and nothing at all unless you
click. SuperMedo Server is responsible for what happens there.

**Backups.** We back up the forum database and uploaded files every night and keep the backups for
14 days, so the forum can be restored after a failure.

**How long we keep data.** Your account and posts stay until you delete them or ask us to. In your forum
settings you can export your data and request the deletion of your account; you can also write to us.

## Your rights

You have the right to access the data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict
its processing, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to receive your data in a
portable format (Art. 15–21 GDPR). To use these rights, write to
[bbport@supermedo.com](mailto:bbport@supermedo.com). You can also complain to a data protection
supervisory authority, for example the one where you live.

## E-mail

If you write to us, we use your message and address only to answer you. Our mailbox is provided by
iFastNet, the host of supermedo.com, which processes the messages on our behalf.
