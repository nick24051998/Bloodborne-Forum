Test builds come here before they are released. Each new build gets its own topic, pinned at the top, with the download, the changes and what to look at. You get a notification when a new one is out.

### How to test

1. Unpack the build into a **new, empty folder**, next to your normal port. Don't overwrite your normal installation.
2. Bring your characters along with **Saves → From other versions**. Your normal saves stay as they are.
3. Play what the topic of the build asks about, and anything else you like.

### How to report

Reply in the topic of the build you tested: what you did, what happened, and **Help → Save a report** attached. One problem per reply makes it easier to follow.

A test build can break things: keep a backup of your saves (**Saves → Backups**).

### Who can see this?

Testers with the Discord role *Beta Hunter* or *Test Subject*, members of the forum group *Beta Testers*, and the team. Want to test? Ask the team on [Discord]({{DISCORD_INVITE_URL}}).
