New to the port? The [best settings page]({{WEBSITE_URL}}/best-settings/) shows the whole first start screen by screen, with pictures of the launcher.

- **The short version:** start `Bloodborne.exe`, pick the goal **Balanced** if you are unsure, take the resolution marked "Recommended", press PLAY.
- **Smooth, Balanced or Quiet:** what each goal does and who it is for.
- **Settings by graphics card:** resolution, upscaler, frame rate and frame generation, from 4 GB cards to 16 GB, and for Intel graphics.
- **If it doesn't run well:** stutter, crashes, black screen and code 3.

Your own numbers help others choose: post them in [Share your FPS](/t/performance).
