Your measurements help other hunters decide what to buy and which settings to use. We also use them to turn the rough guide in the install guide ("Will it run on my PC?" on {{WEBSITE_URL}}/install/) into real numbers.

**How to measure**

1. Use the newest port version and note it.
2. Turn on an FPS counter: the overlay of your graphics driver (NVIDIA app: Alt+R, AMD Software: Ctrl+Shift+O, or Intel's graphics software). Don't use MSI Afterburner or RTSS, they can make the game crash.
3. **Test run:** start at the Central Yharnam lamp and play for about one minute along the main path towards the Great Bridge. Note the average FPS and, if your counter shows it, the 1% low.
4. Optional: stand in the Hunter's Dream in front of the workshop for 30 seconds and note the FPS.

**How to post**

Start a new discussion in Performance Reports; the form fills itself in. A good title is short and complete, for example: `RTX 3060 Ti · Ryzen 7 5700X · 1440p DLSS Quality · 95 FPS`. Tested several resolutions or settings? List them all in the same post.

Please only share screenshots of the game or the launcher, and nothing personal.
