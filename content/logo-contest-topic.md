The bbport launcher gets a new logo, and you can make it. The winning design becomes the new launcher logo, credited with your name.

**See every entry and vote:** [the contest page](/logo-contest)

### Dates

- **Send in your logo until 8 November 2026** (end of the day, UTC).
- **Voting runs from 9 to 15 November 2026.** Every member who is logged in has one vote and can move it until the end. You can't vote for your own logo.

### How to enter

1. On the [contest page](/logo-contest), press **Submit your logo**. It opens a new topic in Logo Contest with a short form.
2. The title is the name of your logo. Say in a sentence or two what it shows.
3. Attach a **PNG preview**: 1024 × 1024 pixels, transparent background. The first picture in your post is the one shown in the gallery.
4. Attach the **source file** in a zip: SVG if you can (vector), otherwise AI, PSD or a large PNG.

Up to three entries per person, one topic for each. Others can comment in your topic.

The forum checks your entry when you post it: the picture has to be a **square PNG with a transparent background**, at least 512 × 512, and the **zip** has to be attached. If something is missing, it tells you what to fix.

### What the logo has to do

The logo is the mark of the launcher: big on the Play page, small in the side bar, and as the Windows icon on the taskbar.

- **Square**, and readable from **32 × 32 pixels** (taskbar) up to **512 × 512** (Play page). Few fine details.
- Works on the **dark launcher background** (#0d0e10). A version for light backgrounds is a bonus.
- **No text needed**: the launcher writes "Bloodborne PC Port" next to it.
- The [template]({{WEBSITE_URL}}/logo-contest-template.svg) shows the canvas and the safe area.

### Rules

- Only your own work. No official Bloodborne, PlayStation or FromSoftware logos, and no art taken from the game.
- By entering, you allow the bbport project to use and adapt your logo in the launcher, on bbport.de, in the forum and on Discord.
- Be kind in the comments. The team removes entries that break the rules.
