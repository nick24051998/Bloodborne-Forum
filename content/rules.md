Welcome, good hunter! This forum belongs to the **Bloodborne PC Port** community. It's the long-form companion to our [Discord server]({{DISCORD_INVITE_URL}}).

### The rules

1. **Be respectful.** No harassment, hate speech or personal attacks. Disagree with ideas, not people.
2. **No piracy.** Do **not** post, request or link to game files, firmware, keys, or any copyrighted material, and don't ask where to get them. Posts like that are removed and the account may be banned.
3. **No malware, no shady downloads.** Mods and patches must link to their original, trustworthy source.
4. **Use the right category.** Something doesn't work for you: *Help & Support*. An idea: *Suggestions*. Tools and tweaks: *Mods & Patches*, tutorials: *Guides*, everything else: *The Hunter's Dream*. You don't post in *Known Fixes* or *Bug Reports*: the team moves confirmed bugs there and adds the fixes that worked.
5. **Search before posting.** Look in *Known Fixes* first, your problem may already have a solution. In *Help & Support*, mark the reply that solved your problem as **Best Answer**.
6. **English only**, so that everyone can help.
7. **No spam or self-promotion** without the staff's permission.

### Asking for help

Starting a topic in **Help & Support** prefills a template. Please fill in every field:

- Port version
- GPU + driver version
- What happens (and what you expected)
- Your **BB Report Tool** zip, attached with the upload button

Topics that leave out this information will be answered slowly or not at all.

Staff may edit, move or remove posts that break these rules. Questions about moderation? Ask the team on Discord.
